This 9-week module introduces bioinformatics concepts and tools relevant for pharmaceutical sciences. Students will gain hands-on experience with public databases and online tools to retrieve, analyse, and interpret molecular databases.

Format:

  • Weekly: 1-hour theory class + 1.5-hour practical class (repeated for 2 student groups).

Target audience: Pharmacy students with introductory knowledge in molecular biology and genetics.

Evaluation:

    1. Participation in class is welcome, and will be positively evaluated.
    1. The details related to the final evaluation format will be discussed and agreed with the students. Either:
    • A final written exam; or
    • A video (3 minutes maximum; in groups of 5 students) + oral presentation (10 min) of one drug target (explored in at least 3 of the molecular databases covered in class).

Goal

Learn to independently explore and critically interpret bioinformatics resources in order to connect drugs with their molecular targets and understand their pharmacological, mechanistic, and clinical relevance.

Learning outcomes

By the end of the TP practical classes, students should be able to:

  • Identify the molecular target(s) of a given drug using authoritative online bioinformatics resources.

  • Retrieve and interpret relevant molecular information on drug classes, mechanisms of action, target function, 3D structure, and clinical associations.

  • Integrate data from multiple sources to explain the pharmacological and clinical importance of a drug–target pair.

  • Demonstrate independent inquiry skills by selecting appropriate databases, justifying search strategies, and reflecting on findings.

  • Communicate discoveries clearly through concise answers.

TP Activities overview

The students will have access to a hands-on guided tutorial to learn how to explore online resources in order to uncover information that is central to pharmaceutical sciences students.

The following databases will be covered:

  • Ensembl = gene identity, functional details, and Genome browsing
  • UniProt = protein details
  • PDB = protein structure
  • Drug bank = drug details
  • ClinPGx = clinical pharmacogenomics
  • Opentargets = therapeutic drug targets


Topic 1 | Introduction to Bioinformatics and Biological Databases

  • Lecture Topics:

    • Definition and scope of bioinformatics.
    • Bioinformatics in drug discovery.
    • Overview of databases per biological data types.
    • Primary and secondary databases.
    • Bioinformatics as an experimental science: search, compare, model, and integrate data.

  • TP Activity:

    • Introductory hands-on tutorial from EBI: Bioinformatics for the terrified

Topic 2 | Genes, Genomes, and Regulation

  • Database: Ensembl

  • Lecture topics

    • Navigating the Ensembl genome browser
    • Understanding gene structure (exons, introns, UTRs, CDS)
    • Finding and exploring gene variants

  • Learning goals

    • Learn to locate a gene in a genome browser.
    • Retrieve basic genomic information such as chromosomal position and gene structure.
    • Interpret visual representations of genes and transcripts.

  • TP Activity

    • Part 1: Introductory video + Hands-on tutorial
    • Part 2: Hands-on tutorial (continuation)

Topic 3 | Proteins

  • Database: UniProt,

  • Lecture topics

  • Learning goals

    • Learn to retrieve and interpret protein functional information.
    • Use UniProt as and entry point to integrate information from variuous sources.

  • TP Activity

    • Hands-on tutorial.
